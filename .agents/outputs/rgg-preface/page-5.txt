Acknowledgements  
This book is the product of several years of independent research and a shared 
conviction that cannabis governance must be understood from the bottom up. 
I owe a profound debt to my co-author and son, Sunny, whose devotion to Lord Shiva, 
Ayurveda, and the healing traditions surrounding this ancient plant shaped the spirit of 
this work. From a young age, he dedicated himself to restoring cannabis to its rightful 
place within communities that have long used, and stewarded it. 
He has witnessed the human costs of prohibition: fractured families, vulnerable 
children, and lives burdened by stigma and humiliation. At the time of publication, he 
remains subject to an unresolved cannabis-related prosecution. Yet his unflinching 
commitment to truth, equity, repair, and justice for the voiceless across borders 
continues to inspire us. This work is, in many ways, the response of a humble global 
citizen to governance failure, cultural erasure, and the unfinished process of 
decolonisation. 
It is also the outcome of a home-grown, family-rooted learning and research initiative.
