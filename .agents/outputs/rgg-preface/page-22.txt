Part IV — Governance and Development Pathways (Chapters 12–17) examines 
regulatory systems, institutional arrangements, and development pathways, with a focus 
on LMIC. It addresses inclusion, equity, and financing within broader development 
strategies. This part argues that durable reform depends on coherent governance 
architectures that align regulatory design, institutional capacity, inclusive participation, 
and sustainable finance. 
Part V — System Design and Futures (Chapters 18–20) turns to implementation and 
forward-looking analysis, considering policy sequencing, data systems, adaptive 
governance, and possible future trajectories of cannabis policy. This part confronts a 
simple but demanding truth: a law is only as durable as the institutions built to give it 
life. Adaptive and informed governance is not a refinement, but the foundation. 
Across all parts, the book maintains a consistent analytical approach based on 
differentiation, institutional readiness, and adaptive governance. It bridges analysis and 
practice by providing rigorous, operationally relevant insights.
